How the estimate works

Your sheet's Attack is your stats plus the equipped weapon's Physical ATK. The page keeps your measured DPS for each weapon and scales it with that weapon's Attack at each level: test Attack − Longsword ATK + the weapon's ATK, then 1.3 per Strength point above or below your test Strength. Strength is a flat bonus, so each point is a bigger share of the Longsword's lower Attack. Not modelled: crit triggers, Sharpwake, Resolve burst, Bleed, cleave on packs, stamina outside your test.

The 2-minute test

  1. Swap weapons once and check that Attack changes by the ATK difference. If not, the model needs changing.
  2. Pick one tough enemy and hit it with your normal single-target loop for 10 seconds per weapon, stamina included.
  3. Add up the damage numbers (a screen recording helps). Do it 2–3 times and average.
  4. Enter both totals, the seconds, and your Attack and Strength at the time. Re-test when you change weapon or tempering.
Plan settings

Warrior base stats at level 1: STR 29, AGI 16, CON 17, INT 10, TAL 11, END 7. From the start level, Talent is taken out of the Strength share until it reaches the weapon requirement. If that would miss level 40, Constitution points are used too.

Longsword trait order

Early Access regions hold 92

Trait points come from Raeven's Coilstones (boss-guarded), not levels. Costs assume a node with two parents needs only one of them, which matches RaidDex's 57-point Longsword build. Skip any node you already have.

    Checks to run in game

    • Sharpwake crit. At 3 Sharpwake stacks (from parries), read your crit chance. If it is 20% or more, crit triggers fire often without Agility.
    • Talent preview. Preview +1 Talent and note the elemental (Wind) attack it adds. If it is large, start Talent earlier in the settings.
    • Equip load. If your armor puts you in the medium or heavy tier, stamina costs rise. Talent raises the load limit.
    • Survival. Dying in boss fights or PvP? Switch the ratio to 1 : 1 or 1 : 2 for a few levels.
